# Fictional local judging and feed

The deterministic BeerJudge targets one fictional pale ale: 35 IBU, OG 1.055, 2.4 CO2 volumes, five categories of 20 points. Scores penalize normalized flaws with visible coefficients; feedback ties each penalty to a brewing action. Severe diacetyl or acetaldehyde means >=0.6 on the simulation's normalized scale. These are game thresholds, not measured sensory concentrations.

SocialPsyopsService validates JudgeResult and triggers only for score <45, severe diacetyl, or severe acetaldehyde. Score 44 triggers; 45 alone does not. A batch id is considered once per service lifetime, including a non-triggering submission; repeated loads in the running game cannot retrigger it. Deduplication is session-local (it resets when the application restarts). The latest triggered feed replaces the previous feed; at most 4096 batch ids are retained and further submissions are declined. New batches use incrementing seeds/ids; replay uses the full saved state.

512 procedural personas are addressable by index. A page materializes exactly 16 dictionaries, and the UI replaces at most 16 rows. Seed + persona index controls name, initial-avatar color and joke selection; the 512 unique handles have explicit sim_ prefixes. Imagery consists solely of colored initial glyphs, with no photos or real people. Every account, statement and scenario is fictional; the interface labels them as simulated. No credentials, delivery configuration, requests, sockets, webhooks or external platform APIs are present. This sandbox is a local game consequence and cannot dispatch harassment to anyone.

Tests exercise boundary scores, independent severe-flaw triggers, once-per-batch behavior, malformed results, all 512 unique personas, page bounds, seed reproduction, category sums and severe thresholds. The full milestone harness also exercises complete brewing and deterministic save continuation. Desktop evidence and actual limitations are recorded in evidence/milestone-review.md.
